# Session behavior

Decided by the owner through live testing. These can change, but ask before "fixing" any of them.

- **Start bell rings on every start, including resume** after a pause.
- **Duration (presets + custom) is locked while running and while paused**, greyed out; Reset unlocks it.
- **Custom length and woodblock times are whole minutes on − / + steppers**: 1 minute at a time up to 10, then 5 at a time (length 1-99, woodblock every 1-30, starting after 1-60). A value between steps (a saved 12) steps to its neighbours; holding a button repeats.
- **Interval bell settings**: locked while running, usable while paused (left open for now).
- **Interval woodblock** (the UI's name for interval bells): "Hit the woodblock every N minutes" (`intervalDuration`, 1-30 min, default 10) and "Starting after N minutes" (`intervalStart`, 1-60 min, default 5) - the first knock comes at the start time, then every interval (default: 5, 15, 25 …). Flipping its switch either way plays one strike as a sample (from the tap, after `unlock()`, so iOS allows it; the switch is locked while running). Code and settings keys still say "interval bell".
- **Volume sliders** are always usable.
- **Test bell** (under the volume sliders): rings the end bell once at the bell volume, whatever `endStrikes` says - a sound check before a sit (loud enough? coming out of the speaker, not someone's earbuds?). From the tap, after `unlock()`, so iOS allows it. Disabled until the sounds have loaded and while a session is running or settling in, so it never rings over a sit; usable while paused.
- **Ambient sound choice** is always usable: *None* stops the sound immediately; another sound switches right away while running, or starts on resume while paused.
- **Ambient sounds download when chosen** (7-25 MB each; nothing up front, so Start is ready within moments), with a progress ring on the button, and are then kept on the device for good (offline too). A sound plays only once it's kept: until then the selector shows *None* - also for a saved choice at page load, which downloads once the bells have loaded and then shows as selected. The choice itself stays saved. A download finishing mid-session starts the sound if it's still the choice (chosen while running: primed in the tap for iOS, and the previous sound stops). A failed download (offline) shows a crossed-out cloud; choosing it again retries.
- **Play after a completed session starts a new full session** (no Reset needed).
- **Reset during a sit is held**: while a session is running or paused, the Reset button has to be held for a second (`HOLD_TO_RESET_MS` in `TimerControls`), filling up as it's held - a stray tap on a phone lying on a cushion shouldn't end a sit. A quick tap, or letting go early, only shows "Hold Reset to end the sit" under the controls for a moment. Enter or Space on the button (a click with `detail` 0), and the R shortcut, reset at once: deliberate, and holding is hard with a screen reader. Outside a sit and while settling in, a tap is enough.
- **"Session N"** shows the session you're on today: completed count + 1, or the just-completed number while "Complete" shows (settling in for the next already shows the next). Memory only; starts over on reload and on a new day. Resets don't count.
- Start is disabled for a 0:00 duration.
- **"Ends at HH:MM"** shows under the timer only while running (hidden when paused, since the end moves); locale time format via `formatClockTime()`.
- **Layout**: title (hidden while a metta, Itipi so or Metta Sutta card shows, so the phrases take its place), (metta mode, in a session) the phrase card, or (Itipi so / Metta Sutta mode) its card, then the time with no card around it - a radiant glow (nimitta) - then the controls on their own card, then - directly below them - any sit notice (`sound-interrupted` with Restore the bell, `screen-may-lock`), then the settings card. The glow's layers reach well past the ring (the page clips them sideways, `overflow-x-clip`) and fade to nothing; there's no ring track, only the progress arc as a trail of light (hidden at 0). It **breathes** - 4 s swelling, 4 s settling (`nimittaBreath`, scale 0.95-1.05, opacity 0.8-1) - only while running or settling in; paused, Ready and Complete hold it still where it is (`animation-play-state`), and there's no animation with reduced motion. The digits themselves stay steady.
- **Quiet screen**: while running, the settings card and keyboard hint are hidden and the page dims (`quiet-dim` overlay) - all but the time and its glow, and the metta, Itipi so or Metta Sutta card, which sit above the dim (`z-20`) and keep shining. Dimming is a setting: `dimScreen` (switch, default on) and `dimLevel` (slider, 10-90% black, default 25%, shown only while dimming is on), under "Visual controls" at the end of the settings; with it off, sitting still hides the settings but leaves the page bright. Changing the level shows the dim for 1.5 s, so it can be set without starting a session; "Show settings" (`aria-expanded`) reveals them and lifts the dim for that run. Paused/stopped shows everything; every start begins quiet again.
- **Settling in** (`settleSeconds`: 0/5/10/20/60, default 0 = off - a saved 30 from before falls back to off; the tap that begins it primes the chosen ambient sound - `audioManager.primeAmbient()`, muted play + pause - since iOS won't let the countdown's timer start an `<audio>` element otherwise): only before a *new* session (from Ready or after completion), never on resume. Silent countdown ("Settling in…"), then the normal start. Counts as in-session: quiet screen, wake lock, duration locked. The main button becomes **Cancel**; Cancel, Space and Reset return to Ready. On completion it calls the *latest* `startTimer` via a ref, so changes made while settling (e.g. ambient sound) apply.
- **Bell patterns**: `startStrikes`, `intervalStrikes`, `endStrikes` (1-3, default 1). Strikes are 5 s apart for start/end bowls, 2 s for the interval woodblock (`BELL_STRIKE_SPACING_MS`). Reset and starting a new session (e.g. Play right after the end bell) cancel strikes not yet rung; pause and resume don't. **Not shown for now** (de-cluttering): `App` doesn't render `BellPatternSettings` (the "Bell strikes" switch, `showBellStrikes`, and its 1×/2×/3× choices), so each bell rings once unless strike counts were saved earlier - saved counts keep applying. The component and its settings are kept (component test in `BellPatternSettings.test.jsx`) for when it comes back.
- **Gentle ending** (`gentleEnding`, default on): while running, the ambient level follows `gentleEndingLevel()` - full until the last minute (or the last half of sessions under 2 min), then linearly to 0. Applied as `audioManager.setAmbientLevel()`, a multiplier separate from the volume slider; back to 1 whenever it doesn't apply.
- **Metta mode** (`mettaMode`, default off; `mettaSeconds` 5/10/20/30, default 10): while a session is running or paused, a separate `MettaCard` above the timer's glow shows one of the four `METTA_PHRASES` (oneself → loved ones → those I find difficult → all beings everywhere), then back to the first. Which phrase is derived from the time sat (`mettaStep()`), so pause holds it and resume carries on. Each phrase fades in, holds and fades out over its time (`mettaFlash` keyframes, frozen while paused; no animation with reduced motion). Hidden when Ready, settling in and after Reset. Never dimmed: the card sits above the quiet screen's dim, like the time. The card has a fixed height so the timer doesn't jump between one- and three-line phrases.
- **Itipi so mode** (`itipisoMode`, default off; `itipisoPace` 1/2/3/5 s per word, default 2; described in the settings only as "Itipi so."): the recollection of the Buddha, Dhamma and Saṅgha (`ITIPISO_LINES`, 14 lines), one line at a time while a session is running or paused - on one card above the glow (`ItipisoCard`): the Pali, then under a rule (`<hr>`) its English. Each line lasts its Pali words × the pace, and each word brightens in turn (`RecitedWords`, `recitationWord` keyframes; each word's delay is set once when the line appears - changing a running animation's delay moves it along its timeline, which once ran the words at double speed; frozen while paused; with reduced motion the whole line shows bright). A rest of 2 words' time after each section and 4 before starting again holds the line just finished on screen. Both halves of the card have fixed heights (sized for the longest line; smaller text under 375px wide), so nothing jumps. To fit the card, the time and the controls on a phone, the page has less padding at the top while a phrase card replaces the title (metta too), and in Itipi so mode the glow drops its phone padding (`TimerDisplay`'s `compact`) - the controls just fit on an iPhone 15 in Safari; not quite on an iPhone SE. Derived from the time sat (`itipisoStep()`), so pause holds it and resume carries on. **Metta, Itipi so and Metta Sutta mode are one at a time** (`PHRASE_MODES`): turning one on turns the others off (a saved clash keeps the first of metta, itipi so, metta sutta). Hidden when Ready, settling in, after Reset and in guided mode; above the quiet screen's dim, like the time. One cycle takes about 2 min at 2 s per word.
- **Metta Sutta mode** (`mettaSuttaMode`, default off; `mettaSuttaPace` 0.5/0.75/1/1.5 s per word, default 0.75 - an unhurried English reading pace, about 3½ min a round): the Karaniya Metta Sutta (Sn 1.8) in the Amaravati Sangha's translation, English only, a couplet (the source's line + indented line; `METTA_SUTTA_COUPLETS`, 22 in two parts) at a time on one card above the glow (`MettaSuttaCard`, 128 px - the longest couplet is 110 px on an iPhone 15), each word brightening in turn, like Itipi so (`utils/recitation.js`: `recitationStep()`, `splitWords()` - a lone dash stays with its word). Each couplet lasts its words and a 1-word breath × the pace, rounded up to whole seconds (the time sat moves in seconds); 3 words' rest after the first part, 4 before beginning again, the couplet staying on screen. Otherwise as Itipi so: pause holds it, hidden when Ready/settling/after Reset/in guided mode, above the dim, one at a time with the other phrase modes. **License**: the translation's license asks for its full text in every copy, so the setting has a small (i) button (`aria-expanded`) that shows the source (linked), the full license and its provenance (`METTA_SUTTA_LICENSE`); its other terms - free of charge, derivatives marked as such - fit the app (free for ever; "Shown here a couplet at a time").
- **Guided meditation** (`guidedMode`, default off; `guidedTrack`, default `metta`): one of four recordings by Thanissaro Bhikkhu (`AUDIO_SOURCES.guided`: Metta 4:09, Breath short 12:16, Breath older 29:32, Breath with leaving 40:00; the settings describe each length in words - "Four minutes", "Half an hour" - not mm:ss) sets the session: Play rings the start bell, the voice begins `GUIDED_LEAD_IN_SECONDS` (15 s) later, and the end bell rings the moment the recording ends - the session lasts exactly 15 s + the recording (`seconds`, measured to the millisecond in Chromium). Nothing else plays: no ambient sound, woodblock, metta phrases, settling in or gentle ending; their settings (and duration/open-ended) are hidden while it's on, and still saved. Pause pauses the voice; resume carries on exactly where the session is (`playAmbient(track, elapsed - 15)`) **without** the start bell, which would ring over the voice; a pause in the lead-in holds it. Recordings download when chosen, like ambient sounds (progress ring, kept offline); Start is disabled until the chosen one is on the device. The switch and choice are locked during a session. `?speed` speeds up the timer but not the voice.
- **Lock screen play/pause** (and headphones'; iOS shows them while the ambient sound or a guided voice plays, `useMediaSession`): they act on the whole session, not just the sound. Pause does what Space does - pauses a running session (clock and sound) or cancels settling in; play only resumes a paused session (with the start bell, as in the app; a guided session without it) - a new session is started in the app. The lock screen shows "Meditating" / "Settling in" / "Paused" over "Wisdom Timer" with the app icon, and nothing once the session is over or reset.
- **Guided session interrupted from outside** (iOS: a phone call, Siri, headphones unplugged): the session pauses where the voice stopped - the voice's position, not the clock, since the page may only wake up at unlock - and shows a deliberately loud screen in place of the time (`InterruptedPause`: **PAUSED** in white outlined in black, one big round **Carry on** button, "Interrupted from outside the app…"). Carry on resumes voice and timer together (a tap, so iOS allows the sound again), without the start bell. If the page wakes past the end with the voice cut short, the session is rescued the same way instead of completing. A lead-in interruption pauses by the clock. Reset clears it. A pause of your own looks as before. **Other sessions run on** through an interruption: the sit's length is the point there.
- **Regular sit interrupted from outside** (iOS: a phone call ringing, Siri, an alarm - the audio context goes `interrupted`): the sit runs on - its length is the point - but iOS may keep the sound off until a tap, even with the context back to "running", so the end bell (and the ambient sound) would stay silent. So while it runs or settles in, a notice shows above the dim (`sound-interrupted`, `role="alert"`): "The sound was interrupted (a call, Siri, an alarm?), so the end bell may not ring." with a **Restore the bell** button. The tap calls `unlock()` (fresh audio, `rebuildAudio()`) and starts the ambient sound again - or, while settling in, primes it again for the countdown - without the start bell. The notice goes on Reset, a new Start (which restores the sound anyway) and at the end; it isn't offered while paused, where the next Start restores the sound.
- **A reload mid-sit** (a pull to refresh, the browser discarding the tab and loading it again): the sit in progress is kept for the tab (`utils/savedSit.js`, `sessionStorage`: its end, in real time, while running - so `?speed` works too; the time left while paused) and picked up on the next load - not in a new tab, and not if the settings that shape the sit (mode, length) changed meanwhile. A running regular or open-ended sit runs on to the same end, with no start bell, and shows the restore notice: "The page reloaded during the sit, so the end bell can't ring until you tap." - audio needs a tap after a load on every browser (**Restore the bell**, waiting for the sounds to load). A paused sit comes back paused with the same time left. A running guided sit comes back paused where it was, on the loud PAUSED screen with Carry on (its voice needs a tap). A sit that ended while the page was away doesn't ring late: Ready shows "Your last sit ended at HH:MM, while the page was reloading." until the next Start or Reset. Reset and the end clear what's kept.
- **Open-ended sitting** (`openEnded`, default off): the timer runs as a countdown from 24 h (`OPEN_ENDED_SECONDS` in `App.jsx`) but displays the time sat (counting up); no progress ring, "Ends at" or gentle ending. **Finish** (`useTimer.finish()`) completes early - end bell, burst, session counted - and keeps the time sat on screen; Play then starts from 00:00. The Duration presets/custom stepper are hidden while it's on (they come back, with the last duration, when it's switched off); the switch itself is locked during a session.
- **Leading a group sit?** (`GroupSitTips`, below the settings card; not on the quiet screen): a native `<details>`, folded by default, with six steps in order for whoever leads a group - set up with a connection (the sound downloads when chosen; then the app works offline), keep calls away (airplane mode with Wi-Fi off, or Do Not Disturb; silent mode is fine), charge the phone, ring the Test bell last (so it checks the sit's real conditions - silent switch, volume, speaker), don't lock the phone, and Restore the bell if the sound is interrupted anyway. The README has the same list.
- **Keep screen awake**: always on - a wake lock is held only while the timer is *running* (or settling in), not while paused, where the Wake Lock API is supported. It's asked for again when the page becomes visible, and when the system lets go of it while visible. **If the screen can't be kept on** - no Wake Lock API, or the request refused (e.g. low battery) - a warning shows during the sit, above the dim (`screen-may-lock`): "The screen may lock by itself - keep it on, or the end bell may not ring on time." On touch screens (`pointer-coarse:`), a line under the controls outside a sit (`lock-hint`) says "Don't lock the phone while you sit: a locked phone may not ring the end bell on time." - or, without the API, to turn off auto-lock for the sit. Its switch (`KeepAwakeSetting`) isn't shown for now, and `keepScreenAwake` is ignored (still saved, so an earlier "off" comes back if the switch does).
